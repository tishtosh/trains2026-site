About This Catalog

This website shows model trains from a private collection. The owner wants to sell these trains.

All photos on this website are original and have not been retouched or edited in any way. What you see in each photo is exactly what the train looks like.

We used a computer program to help us identify the model, manufacturer and other details. This program is called AI, short for artificial intelligence. It looked at photos of each train and made a guess about it.

The AI guessed things like the maker, the size, and the age of each train. Each guess has a percent number next to it, like 90%. A higher number means the AI is more sure. A lower number means the AI is less sure.

AI guesses can be wrong. Please do not treat every guess as fact. Look closely at the photos. If you know trains well, check the details yourself.

Some pages also have notes about value or price. These notes are casual guesses, not real facts. If the value matters to you, ask a trained expert to look at the train in person.

Before you buy anything, please ask questions. Look at all the photos closely.

Thank you for visiting this collection.
